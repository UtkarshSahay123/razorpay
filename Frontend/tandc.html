Policies

Course Return Policy

At Lumina Education, we want you to be completely satisfied with your learning experience. Our return policy for digital courses and physical materials is designed to be fair and transparent.

Digital Courses

Digital courses can be "returned" (canceled) within 14 days of purchase, provided that less than 20% of the course content has been accessed or completed.

  • Requests must be submitted via the Support Center.
  • Course access will be revoked immediately upon processing.

Physical Materials

Physical learning materials, such as workbooks or equipment, must be returned within 30 days of receipt.

  • Items must be in original, unused condition.
  • Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the student unless the item was defective.

Refund Guidelines

Our refund process is straightforward and aims to process eligible requests promptly.

Processing Times

Once a return or cancellation is approved, refunds are initiated within 2-3 business days.

Please note that it may take an additional 5-10 business days for the funds to appear on your original payment method, depending on your financial institution.

Non-Refundable Items

  • Completed certification exams.
  • Custom coaching sessions that have already occurred.
  • Digital downloads (e.g., PDF templates) once accessed.

Privacy & Data Security

Your privacy is paramount. This section details how Lumina Education handles your personal and transaction data.

Data Collection

We collect essential information required to provide our educational services, process payments, and improve your learning experience. This includes account details, course progress, and secure payment tokens.

Data Protection

All transactions are encrypted using industry-standard SSL technology. We do not store raw credit card numbers on our servers; they are securely handled by our PCI-compliant payment partners.
