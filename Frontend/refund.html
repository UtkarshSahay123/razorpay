How to Request a Refund

To initiate a refund, please follow these steps:

  1. Navigate to your Account Settings.
  2. Select the Billing & Subscriptions tab.
  3. Locate the specific course or subscription and click Request Refund.
  4. Fill out the brief feedback form to help us improve.

info Refunds typically process within 5-10 business days depending on your financial institution.
